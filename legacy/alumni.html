Volunteer and mentor at UU

You can continue to support the UU and help our students thrive by volunteering with us. Your knowledge, experience, expertise, support and time are invaluable in helping us promote UU’s unique mission and improve the experience of current students. Volunteering also helps you remain a part of the UU community, stay involved in the life of the UU, and even develop new skills and make new friends. Volunteers inspire and support the career ambitions of students by sharing their industry insight and providing career guidance.

Make a gift for UU

If you are in a position to make a gift to support UU you can make:

1. Donation for poor student scholarship

Donor support helps us respond quickly to our student's needs, providing the services and interventions that make an immediate difference to their studies and have a lasting impact on their lives.

By Post: We accept cheques and postal orders. Simply send your gift to the Office of External Affairs, and we will write back to confirm the receipt. Remember to make all cheques payable to ‘Uttara University’.

2. Donation for UU development

You can donate to Uttara University's development. Cheques should be made payable to ‘Uttara University’ and sent to the Office of External Affairs.

3. Donation for the time of national crisis

(Any natural disaster, given that UU is participating as a fund-raiser)

Uttara University is ready to provide various benefits and services to UU alumni. As a UU graduate, you can get access to the following benefits:

1. Alumni Corporate Discount

UU provides an exclusive free alumni card. This card covers several corporate discounts and premier health service discount packages from renowned healthcare organizations.

2. Alumni Discount at UU

UU alumni are eligible to receive a tuition fee discount when he/she is enrolling in a Master’s degree. For more information, please visit Tuition & Fees.

3. Career Support from UU Centre for Career Development

As a former student, you can attend free events, workshops, and seminars run by our careers support and employability team, UU Centre for Career Development, book one-to-one appointments for career advice, and search and apply for jobs. As an Alumni, you are also eligible to attend all kinds of soft and hard skills development training programs usually arranged by UU.
