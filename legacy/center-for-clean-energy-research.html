About

The Center for Clean Energy Research (CCER) is a shining example of innovation in the academic world, originating from the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE). Its creation was approved by the 63rd Academic Council on December 28, 2023, and then endorsed by the 38th Syndicate on March 9, 2024. This significant approval marks a new era in the institution's commitment to pioneering research in the field of sustainable energy. It is a testament to the institution's steadfast dedication to advancing knowledge and technology in clean energy solutions. Its establishment signals a strategic effort to consolidate and streamline research endeavors within the department, fostering an environment that promotes interdisciplinary collaboration and groundbreaking discoveries.

The CCER has the noble aim of facilitating the seamless execution of research endeavors. It provides a centralized platform for scholars, scientists, and students to catalyze the development of innovative technologies and methodologies to address pressing challenges in energy sustainability. Through its multifaceted approach, the CCER harnesses the collective expertise of its members to explore novel avenues in clean energy research. From renewable sources to energy-efficient technologies, the center serves as a crucible for transformative ideas and impactful solutions that will shape the future landscape of energy production and consumption.

Vision

Envisioning a resilient and vibrant future powered by clean energy, the Center for Clean Energy Research aspires to be at the forefront of transforming the global energy landscape through renewable energy technologies, contributing to a sustainable, equitable, and carbon-neutral world.

Mission

Innovate for Impact: To lead in renewable energy research and innovation, developing technologies that are pivotal in the shift towards a sustainable energy ecosystem.

Empower through Partnerships: To build and nurture partnerships that enhance our research capabilities, share knowledge, and drive the implementation of clean energy solutions.

Educate for Sustainability: To educate and empower stakeholders, from researchers to the public, fostering a deep understanding of clean energy’s role in socio-economic development and environmental stewardship.

Director’s Message

As the Director of the Center for Clean Energy Research, I am thrilled to welcome you to an institution where innovation meets impact and sustainability is at the core of everything we do. Our commitment to pioneering research in solar energy and clean technologies is unwavering, as we understand the critical role these play in shaping a sustainable future for our planet.

Our Vision is clear : We aim to be the catalysts for change in the global energy narrative, transitioning from traditional energy sources to a world where clean, renewable energy is not just a possibility, but a reality. We believe in a future where every home, business, and community can access affordable and reliable solar energy, contributing to a carbon-neutral economy and a healthier environment.

Our Mission is threefold:

Innovation: We are dedicated to breaking new ground in solar energy research, pushing the boundaries of what’s possible, and turning innovative ideas into real-world solutions.

Collaboration: We understand that our challenges are complex and require collective effort. That’s why we prioritize building strong partnerships across academia, industry, and government to harness the power of collaboration.

Education: We are committed to educating not just our students but also the wider community, empowering them with the knowledge and tools to make informed decisions about energy use and to advocate for clean energy policies.

As we embark on this journey, I invite you to join us in making a difference. Whether you’re a student, researcher, industry professional, or simply an individual passionate about clean energy, there’s a place for you here at the Center for Clean Energy Research. Together, we can light up the world with the power of the sun and drive the transition to a sustainable energy future.

Warm Regards,
