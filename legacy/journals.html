Introduction

UU Journal of Business, Arts and Social Sciences is an annual academic journal published by Uttara University. The journal provides a platform for researchers and scholars to share their original research, reviews, and case studies in the fields of business, arts, and social sciences. The journal is committed to publishing high-quality research that contributes to the advancement of knowledge in these fields.

Scope

The UU Journal of Business, Arts and Social Sciences publishes original research, reviews, and case studies on a wide range of topics related to business, arts, and social sciences. The scope of the journal includes, but is not limited to, the following topics: business administration, economics, finance, marketing, accounting, management, organizational behavior, international business, entrepreneurship, human resource management, art history, visual arts, performing arts, literature, language, applied linguistics, philosophy, history, anthropology, sociology, political science, and psychology.
