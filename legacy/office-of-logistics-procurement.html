Office of Logistics & Procurement

The Office of Logistics and Procurement at Uttara University serves as a pivotal administrative unit responsible for overseeing the university's logistics operations and procurement processes. With a focus on efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and compliance, our office plays a crucial role in ensuring the seamless flow of goods and services to support the university's academic and operational needs.

At the core of our mission is the objective of managing logistics and procurement activities in a manner that maximizes value and minimizes risk for the university. We work diligently to optimize supply chain processes, streamline procurement procedures, and negotiate favorable contracts to support the academic and administrative functions of the university.

The key responsibilities of the Office of Logistics and Procurement encompass a wide range of functions, including inventory management, vendor selection and management, contract negotiation, and procurement policy development and compliance. We collaborate closely with university departments and units to understand their procurement needs and requirements and to ensure timely and cost-effective acquisition of goods and services.

Furthermore, our office promotes transparency, fairness, and ethical conduct in all procurement activities. We adhere to established procurement policies and procedures and uphold the highest standards of integrity and accountability in our interactions with vendors and suppliers.

In addition to our procurement functions, the Office of Logistics and Procurement also plays a vital role in managing logistics and distribution operations to ensure the timely delivery of goods and services to the university community. We coordinate transportation, storage, and distribution activities to optimize efficiency and minimize disruptions.
