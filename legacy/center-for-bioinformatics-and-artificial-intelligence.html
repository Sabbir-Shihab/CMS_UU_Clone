Message from the Chief Advisor

Welcome to the Center for Bioinformatics and Artificial Intelligence, a milestone in the journey of Uttara University in making itself a hub of cutting-edge research and innovation. We are at the crossroads of biological sciences and computational intelligence, ready to address some of the most critical challenges in healthcare, agriculture, and environmental sustainability.

The Center is a reflection of our commitment to be in the forefront of the Fourth Industrial Revolution. By combining these two powerful fields, our center will foster interdisciplinary research that will uncover complex biological data, develop intelligent systems, and deliver impactful and sustainable outcomes.

We at CBIAI are dedicated to establishing a connection between academia and industry by collaborating with healthcare institutions, research organizations, and technology companies worldwide. Our curriculum prioritizes the application of machine learning and the deployment of AI in healthcare and related disciplines, as well as the analysis of multi-omics data, drug discovery, and prescriptive modelling.

Additionally, we provide diploma programs, short courses, and specialized research paths. We offer practical training to faculty, stakeholders, and, most importantly, the younger generation in order to prepare them for the evolving bioinformatics and AI landscape.

Moreover, our team of expert analysts and researchers provides a comprehensive array of specialized services, including advanced molecular dynamics simulation, whole-genome sequencing (WGS) analysis, and related computational biology support.

I extend my heartfelt thanks to the University administration, our academic partners and well- wishers home and abroad for their continued support.

Center for Bioinformatics and Artificial Intelligence (CBIAI)

Uttara University

The Center for Bioinformatics and Artificial Intelligence (CBIAI) was established with the objective of conducting research and teaching on computational biology. Subsequently, the Center will implement Bioinformatics, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence in the life sciences, thereby transforming UU into a world-class location.

Bioinformatics (BI)

An interdisciplinary field that integrates biology, computer science, mathematics, and statistics to collect, store, analyse, and interpret biological data is known as bioinformatics (BI). It creates computational methods and software to comprehend intricate biological systems via data generated from genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and other life science studies.

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broader discipline of computer science that is dedicated to the development of systems that are capable of performing tasks that are typically associated with human intelligence, including language comprehension, language learning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making.

Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of artificial intelligence that allows computers to generate predictions or decisions by learning patterns from data without requiring explicit programming for each task. As they are exposed to a greater volume of data, machine learning algorithms enhance their performance.

Vision

CBIAI's objective is to establish itself as a leading center of excellence in bioinformatics and artificial intelligence by fostering interdisciplinary research, instruction, and scientific development by integrating biological and computer sciences.

Activities

Advanced research that concentrates on life science is followed by the integration of Bioinformatics, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. Additionally, it will be responsible for the development of training, collaboration, and professional services in computational biology.

Organogram

Responsibilities

1. Vice Chancellor (Patron): The most esteemed position will oversee the overall operations and provide guidance. Decides on the recruitment of personnel and the approval of proposed activities.

2. Director: Will determine the activities that will be conducted and assist the Patron in determining the necessary personnel and activities for approval.

3. Advisory Panel: The Director may request advice from the Advisory Panel regarding the plan of work and the mode of activities.

4. Research Associates: Execute research and any other tasks as determined by the Director and the Advisory Panel.

Key Functions and Objectives

  • Integrate bioinformatics, artificial intelligence, and machine learning to address challenges in medicine, agriculture, and the environment, thereby advancing interdisciplinary research.
  • Create computational tools and analytical pipelines that are AI-driven for the analysis of genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, structural biology, and multi-omics data.
  • To establish a team of educators and students who possess the necessary skills to conduct research and provide support for their ideas both domestically and internationally.
  • Develop national capacity by offering students, researchers, and professionals top-tier academic programs, workshops, and training in bioinformatics, AI, data science, and life sciences.
  • Accelerate scientific discovery and technology transfer by fostering innovation and collaboration through partnerships with universities, research institutes, hospitals, government organisations, and industry.
  • Install high-performance computing facilities, secure biological data repositories, and cloud-based platforms to support advanced life science research and evidence-based decision-making.
  • To provide researchers, industries, and research institutes with professional, fee-based services in computational biology and machine learning.

Note: Functions are subject to modification during the ongoing development process.
