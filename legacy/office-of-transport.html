Office of Transport

The Office of Transport at Uttara University serves as a vital administrative unit responsible for overseeing transportation services and ensuring the safe and efficient movement of students, faculty, and staff within the university community. With a focus on reliability, accessibility, and sustainability, our office plays a pivotal role in providing transportation solutions that meet the diverse needs of the university population.

At the core of our mission is facilitating convenient and accessible transportation options for members of the university community. We strive to ensure that students, faculty, and staff have reliable access to transportation services that support their academic, professional, and personal needs.

The key responsibilities of the Office of Transport encompass a range of functions, including managing university-owned vehicles, coordinating shuttle services, and overseeing parking facilities. We work diligently to optimize transportation routes and schedules, minimize wait times, and maximize the efficiency of transportation operations.

Furthermore, our office is dedicated to promoting sustainable transportation practices and reducing the university's environmental footprint. In addition to our transportation management functions, the Office of Transport also plays a vital role in ensuring the safety and security of transportation services. We implement and enforce safety protocols, conduct vehicle inspections, and provide training to drivers to ensure adherence to safety standards and regulations.
