Scholarship policy Fall 2026 revised from Summer 2026, 6th july 2026 onwards.

The following policy has been issued for Fall 2026 semester admission, effective from 06.07.2026 by revising the Summer 2026 scholarship policy.

1. Scholarship on Results:
  1. 1.1. Founder Scholarship: (Refer to Founder Scholarship policy on website) Students with a Double GPA 5.00 can be awarded scholarships as follows:
    1. a) Students with a GPA 5.00 in both SSC & HSC [with/without optional] will be awarded with 100% scholarship. Applicable for the students passing HSC within the last 3 years [HSC from 2023 onwards and SSC from 2021 onwards].
    2. b) If any student gets an 'A' grade in 2 subjects at O level and passes at A level will be awarded with 100% scholarship. Applicable for the students passing A level exam A level from 2017 onwards and O level from 2015 onwards].
    3. c) Total cost for Founder Scholarship is up to 1,50,000 BDT including all fees*
  1. 1.2. Merit-Based Scholarship:
    1. a) Students who passed SSC in 2018 or earlier and HSC in 2020 or earlier will not receive any scholarship.
    2. b) Students with a GPA 5.00 in SSC and HSC will receive a flat 50% scholarship or maximum offer if exceed current offer. [SSC passing year 2019 & HSC passing year 2021].
    3. c) Students with a single GPA 5.00 either in SSC or HSC will receive a 15% Scholarship. [SSC passing year 2019 & HSC passing year 2021].

2. Students who have completed both O Level and A levels can get a 90% discount on admission fees.

3. All Hon's Program students will get an additional 15%-25% scholarship (Except Civil, Textile, EEE and Math) for Fall 2026.

  1. 3.1. Students in the Dept. of Civil, Textile, EEE and Math for the BSc Program will get a 50%-52% scholarship, for Fall 2026.

4. Diploma Holders for all Regular programs will get merit-based scholarships that are clarified in clause no. 1.2.

  1. 4.1. Scholarship for all students for Fall 2026 will be fixed. The total fees will be for CSE Evening 250,944 BDT, FDT Evening 247,320 BDT, Textile evening 2,31,456 BDT, Civil Evening 255,392 BDT and EEE evening program 2,54,890 BDT (Approx.).

5. All the students of Master's Programs of MSc in CSE, MSc in Math (1 year), MSc in Physics (1 year), MA in Islamic Studies (1 year) will get a 40% scholarship, MBA (for BBA Graduates) will get a 35% scholarship, MSc in Math (2 years), MSc in Physics (2 years) will get a 50% scholarship, MA in Islamic Studies (2 years) will get a 45% scholarship, MA in English (1 & 2 years), ELT (1 & 2 years), LLM (1 year) will get a 35% scholarship.

  1. 5.1. All the students of Master's Programs of MBA Regular (2 years), Executive MBA will get a 50% scholarship, MBA in AMFM (1 year) will get a 60% scholarship, MBA in AMFM (2 years) will get a 58% scholarship, MA in Bengali (1 & 2 years) will get a 55% scholarship.

6. BPEd, MPEd, BEd , MEd Program students will get a 45% Scholarship.

7. Siblings & Spouses

  1. 7.1. If siblings or spouses are admitted together, they will receive an additional scholarship: 5% for Hon’s and Master's students, 2% for Diploma students.
  2. 7.2. For students who are admitted later (after their sibling or spouse has already been admitted), the following additional scholarships will apply: 5% for Hon’s and Master's students, 2% for Diploma students.

8. Alumni and Family: Eligible for a 5% scholarship (Hons and Masters) & 2% scholarship (Diploma Holder).

9. Physically challenged, ethnic minority groups and students of third gender may be granted a special scholarship of a minimum 30% on a case-to-case basis subject to submission of appropriate documents, if no other scholarship is applicable.

10. A 5% additional scholarship will be awarded to female students. [Except BSc programs for Diploma holders and LLB program in addition to the current scholarship and resultbased scholarship].

Note: Only one offer can be availed from 7 to 10

11. A maximum of 50% scholarship can be awarded to students seeking admission from sports and cultural fields subject to submission & verification of appropriate & relevant documents.

12. From partner & affiliated institutions, an additional 5% scholarship for Hon’s and Master’s students, while 2% scholarship for Diploma students can be provided if any teacher from those institutes admits a student with appropriate credentials along with an existing referral, if any.

13. If a student is admitted with reference of a teacher/officer/employee of Uttara University, the amount allotted for reference can be deducted from the student's admission fee together with a special scholarship (if any).

Note:

  1. 1. Any case/situation/condition apart from the above-mentioned clauses will be on a handled case-to-case basis.
  2. 2. In any situation, the admission fee, once paid, is non-refundable.
  3. 3. For program change, TK 5,000/- will be charged once the student ID is created. Also applicable for students who change shift from regular to evening or vice versa.
  4. 4. The total scholarship is capped at 60%.
  5. 5. Students who pay all fees for both the first and second semesters at once will receive an extra 5% scholarship. Those who pay all fees for only the first semester will get an extra 2% scholarship.

*For further information on scholarships, please refer to the 'Scholarship Chart' and 'Fee Structure' available on the website.
