Overview

The Center for Research and Training (CRT) at Uttara University is dedicated to fostering a vibrant academic environment that supports high-quality research and professional development. Our center is committed to advancing knowledge, facilitating innovation, and nurturing the academic growth of our faculty, students, and staff. Through comprehensive training programs and a robust research agenda, CRT aims to contribute significantly to the academic community and society at large.

Mission

To promote and support high-impact research and professional training that addresses contemporary challenges, enhances research-based knowledge, and fosters sustainable development.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in research and training, recognized for its innovative approaches, collaborative spirit, and significant contributions to academic and societal advancement.

History

Founded in 2010, the Center for Research and Training at Uttara University has grown into a pivotal institution dedicated to research excellence and professional development. Over the years, CRT has initiated numerous research projects, organized a wide array of training programs, and collaborated with various national and international institutions and organizations. Our history is marked by a commitment to fostering a research culture that encourages intellectual curiosity and innovative thinking.

Research

The CRT is committed to promoting cutting-edge research across various disciplines. We invite researchers to submit proposals for innovative projects that address critical issues and contribute to knowledge advancement.

Training

The CRT offers a range of training programs designed to enhance professional skills and academic knowledge. Our training sessions include workshops, seminars, certification courses, and more.

Resources

CRT provides access to a wealth of resources to support research and training activities. These include a digital library, research tools, contemporary software, and annual research grant for innovative projects. We are dedicated to equipping our researchers and trainees with the necessary tools to succeed.

Past Events

Our past events have been instrumental in shaping the academic and professional landscape. Highlights include international conferences, symposiums, and specialized workshops that have drawn participants from around the globe.

Reward Policy for Research and Publications (Revised & Valid from 1.1.2026)

Uttara University (UU) encourages high-quality research contributions from its faculty members and staff. To promote impactful publications in high-indexed journals, UU has revised its Reward Policy for Research and Publications, effective for all articles published on or after January 1, 2026.

Reward Policy 1 (No APC)

Each journal article or book chapter published in a regular/special issue by a UU faculty member and staff that is indexed in Scopus or Web of Science and involved no article processing charge (APC), will receive a cash reward based on the quartile as follows:

Reward Policy 2 (With APC)

Each journal article, book chapter, or conference proceeding published in a regular/special issue by a UU faculty member and staff that is indexed in Scopus or Web of Science and involved article processing charge (APC), a maximum APC up to USD 200 will be funded by UU to either the corresponding or first or second author with UU affiliation. Reward Policy 3 (Conference Participation)

If any UU faculty member or staff wants to participate at any international conference (physical, online or hybrid mode) indexed in Scopus or Web of Science database, will receive a grant up to USD 100 to partially cover up the cost of registration fee, transport allowance (if physical) etc. If the proceedings of that conference or selected papers of that proceedings are published in a special/regular issue of a journal and indexed in Scopus or Web of Science, then s/he can claim the Reward Policy 2. Only one person (presenter or attendee of the conference/corresponding or first author of the proceedings) from the author list will receive the cash reward as per Reward Policy 2.

Eligibility Criteria

  • The applicant must be a full-time UU faculty member or staff at the time of application.
  • The article must be published in a journal indexed by Scopus or Web of Science (for details, please contact the Office of the CRT). Journals indexed in other databases will not be considered for the reward.
  • For any article authored by multiple UU faculty members and staff, the award money will be shared by a maximum of three UU members as listed in the authors’ serial number. If the corresponding author is not from UU, the first three UU authors from the list can apply for the reward.
  • To be eligible for the reward, the applicant must use his/her UU affiliation and official email address in the publication.
  • The person can apply for the reward once the article is published and assigned a DOI. Articles catalogued as “forthcoming” or “article in press” are not eligible for a cash reward.
  • Any Web of Science or Scopus-indexed article published on or after January 1, 2026 is eligible for the cash reward.
  • Either “full paper”, “research article” or “review paper” publications are considered for the cash reward, provided these are Web of Science or Scopus-indexed. Publications such as “Letters to the Editor”, “Editorial”, Communiqués, etc. do not qualify for the reward.

Reward Policy for Current UU Students

Any current UU student, journal publication indexed in Scopus or Web of Science, will receive a cash reward of BDT 10,000 for single author and BDT 15,000 for multiple authors. A maximum of two UU students will be eligible for the reward and they need to be corresponding/first/second authors (After second authors, none will be considered). Faculty members or staff, if involved in the publication as co-author, will not receive any cash reward irrespective of the authorship position.
