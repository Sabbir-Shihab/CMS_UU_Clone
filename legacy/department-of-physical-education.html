What is Physical Education, in Health & Sports?

Physical Education is an interdisciplinary academic discipline that examines human movement, physical activity, and sport from scientific, educational, and health-oriented perspectives. It integrates knowledge from health sciences, sports sciences, and social sciences to promote physical fitness, motor development, psychological well-being, and lifelong engagement in physical activity.

As a field of study, Physical Education encompasses core areas such as exercise physiology, biomechanics, sports psychology, motor learning, sports nutrition, health education, and physical activity epidemiology. These areas provide a scientific foundation for understanding how physical activity influences health, performance, and quality of life across different populations.

Research plays a central role in Physical Education as an academic discipline. Through empirical investigation, laboratory experimentation, and field-based studies, the discipline generates evidence to improve teaching practices, training methods, injury prevention strategies, and public health interventions. Research in Physical Education addresses critical issues such as physical inactivity, lifestyle diseases, athletic performance enhancement, youth development, and inclusive participation in sport and physical activity.

By combining theory, research, and practice, Physical Education prepares professionals, educators, and researchers who can apply scientific knowledge to educational institutions, sports organizations, healthcare settings, and community programs. As an academic discipline, it contributes significantly to the promotion of health, the advancement of sports performance, and the development of sustainable and evidence-based physical activity systems.

Bachelor and Masters in Physical Education

In physical education health and sports, students learn physical education theory and methodology, along with practical skills in games, athletics, gymnastics and swimming. The Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) program at Uttara University prepares students to become physical education teachers and sports professionals, teaching them the history of physical education, sports psychology, physiology, and rules of games.

The Master of Physical Education (MPEd) program provides advanced training in exercise physiology, sports science, sports medicine, and research methods. Students gain hands-on experience through labs, practice teaching, and research projects. The curriculum emphasizes the importance of physical education, covers the types of physical education, and organizes courses using a physical education subject code, preparing graduates for careers in physical education colleges, coaching and health promotion, all at an affordable tuition fee.

Department Overview

The Department of Physical Education at Uttara University offers a one-year (three semesters) Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) program and a one-year (three semesters) Master of Physical Education (MPEd) program. The total credit requirement for the BPEd is 44 credit hours. The program consists of 12 theoretical courses, of which 10 are core courses (23 credit hours), and 2 are General Education (GED) courses (6 credit hours), along with seven practical courses (15 credit hours), conducted both in the classroom and in the field. This program provides modern concepts of physical education, preparing BPEd graduates for employment in professional bodies and educational institutions, such as universities and higher secondary and secondary levels. Upon completion of the program, students may pursue an MPEd and other related higher degrees to expand and enrich their knowledge and skills in physical education, building careers as professionals, academicians, and researchers.

The total credit requirement for the MPEd is 42 credit hours. The program consists of 12 theoretical courses, of which 10 are core courses (30 credit hours), and 2 are General Education (GED) courses (6 credit hours), along with two practical courses (6 credit hours), conducted both in the classroom and in the field. This program provides modern concepts of physical education, preparing MPEd graduates for employment in professional bodies and educational institutions, such as universities and higher secondary and secondary levels. Upon completion of the program, students may pursue an MPhil, Ph.D., and other related higher degrees to expand and enrich their knowledge and skills in physical education, building careers as professionals, academicians, and researchers.

The courses emphasise fundamental skills development, social contribution, critical thinking, and personal development in Health, Physical Education, and Sports Science. They typically feature a strong focus on experimentation, methods, demonstration, communication, and interacting effectively to share ideas and experiences with the community and work individually or as part of a team in a professional capacity.

Description of the Program

Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd)

The Department of Physical Education at Uttara University offers a one-year (three semesters) Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd) program. The total credit requirement for the BPEd is 44 credit hours. The program consists of 12 theoretical courses, of which 10 are core courses (23 credit hours) and 2 are General Education (GED) courses (6 credit hours), along with seven practical courses (15 credit hours) conducted both in the classroom and in the field.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The educational objectives of the BPEd program are to ensure that graduates are fundamentally strong, physically and mentally fit, practical, dependable, and professional. Successful graduates will meet the following educational objectives.

PEO1: A broader knowledge base in physical education and sports studies;

PEO2: Leadership skills to utilize in the field of physical education and sports;

PEO3: An ability to research the field of physical education and sports;

PEO4: A conceptual perception of issues affecting the future of sports and physical education in Bangladesh.

Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

After the completion of the BPEd program students will be able to

PLO 1: familiar with modern concepts of physical education and sports;

PLO 2: recognize their personal cognitive, affective, and psychomotor development;

PLO 3: interpret basic physiological systems;

PLO 4: prepare an effective lesson plan to administer classes;

PLO 5: apply coaching methods to prepare school athletes’ competitions;

PLO 6: prepare and implement sports budget for institutions;

PLO 7: organise intramural and extramural games and sports;

PLO 8: develop the human condition's body, mind, and behavioural aspects.

Description of the Program

Master of Physical Education (MPEd)

The Department of Physical Education at Uttara University offers a one-year (three semesters) Master of Physical Education (MPEd) program. The total credit requirement for the MPEd is 42 credit hours. The program consists of 12 theoretical courses, of which 10 are core courses (30 credit hours) and 2 are General Education (GED) courses (6 credit hours), along with two practical courses (6 credit hours) conducted both in the classroom and in the field.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

The educational objectives of the MPEd program are to ensure that graduates are fundamentally strong, physically and mentally fit, practical, dependable, and professional. Successful graduates will meet the following educational objectives.

PEO1: A greater understanding of experiment-based studies on health, physical education, and sports.

PEO2: Development of analyzing capacity, creativity and skills to utilize in the field of physical educationand sports.

PEO3: An ability to research the field of physical education and sports.

PEO4: A conceptual perception of issues affecting the future of Bangladesh's sports, physical education and society.

PLO 1: achieve and develop contemporary Knowledge of Physical Education and Sports.

PLO 5: demonstrate skilful performance in physical education content areas and health-enhancing fitness levels.

PLO 6: apply the modern concept in different teaching methods and job sectors.

PLO 7: understanding the historical, social and psychological aspects of activity and sport in society.

PLO 8: gather an expert knowledge of statistics and computer fundamentals as a general education course for research findings.

Admission Requirements (Graduate)

Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd)

Structure of the Curriculum

a) Duration of the Program: Year: 1, Semester: 3

b) Admission Requirements: Graduates (BA/BSS/BCom/BBS/BSc, BAg.) or holders of a Master of Social Science/Arts, Physical/Biological Science, with at least one-second division from SSC to degree level, are eligible to apply. This condition will be relaxed for serving teachers with M.P.O. Additionally, candidates must be physically and mentally fit. They must qualify for both the written test and viva. The maximum age is 35 for non-teachers and 40 for teachers with M.P.O. If a potential student has a minimum of 10 years of teaching experience or six years of athletic experience at the national level, they will also be considered for the BPEd program.

c) Total Class Weeks in a Year: : 28 Weeks in a Year / 14 Weeks in a Semester (Excluding other activities and assessment).

The total number of courses required for awarding a BPEd degree is 19, and the total credits are 44.

d) Minimum CGPA requirements for graduation: CGPA 2.0

e) Maximum Academic Year of Completion: Within (1+2)= 3 academic years.

g) Category of Courses:

  1. General Education Course: 2 Courses
  2. Core Theoretical Course: 10 Courses
  3. Core Practical Course: 7 Courses

g) Year/ Level/ Semester/ Term wise courses: Spring Semester : (January - April), Summer Semester : (May - August) and Fall Semester : (September - December)
