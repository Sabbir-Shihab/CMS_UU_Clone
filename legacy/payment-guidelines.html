Payment Procedure

All payments should be made through Mobile Banking (bKash, rocket, Nagad) /Debit or Credit Card (VISA, MASTER, NEXUS etc.)/designated Bank branches mentioned in the list. Cash payments will not be accepted by the Office of Finance and Accounts (F&A). Students can pay with an auto-generated deposit slip printed from ERP. They can also collect printed deposit slips from the Office of Finance & Accounts. A deposit slip will not be required in case of mobile banking or card payments.

4. POS Machine:

You can pay Tuition & other Fees through POS Machine using debit/credit cards at the Office of Finance & Accounts (standard charge is applicable by the service provider).

5. Cash Payment:

The Office of Finance & Accounts DOES NOT accept cash deposits for tuition/other fees. Only new students' admission fees are received in cash.
