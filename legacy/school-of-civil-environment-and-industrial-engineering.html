Dean's Message

The School of Civil, Environment and Industrial Engineering is one of the five schools of Uttara University and was established in 2012. The school comprises of the following three dynamic departments:

1. Department of Civil Engineering
2. Department of Fashion Design and Technology
3. Department of Textile Engineering

Our school is committed to quality education, impactful research, academic accreditation and nurturing leadership skills to meet the highest standard. Our enduring success at the school is a testament to the collective spirit and unwavering commitment of our students, faculty members, staff, and alumni. The faculty members of this school maintain high professional, intellectual and ethical standards and are actively engaged in teaching, research and advisory services. We are excited to introduce a fresh approach to industrial partnerships, engaging key stakeholders in advancing education and research and empowering graduates to lead in national and regional large-scale infrastructures and garment industries. We seek to produce highly qualified engineers, professionals and leaders of social relevance with the requisite skills and knowledge to meet the ever-changing needs of society.

A heartfelt thanks to our dedicated faculty members and supportive alumni whose concerted efforts are in harmony with Uttara University’s motto - Excellence in Higher Education and Research. We look forward to working in the coming months to forge an even stronger community and shape a brighter future together. I wish the school every success.

Professor Dr. Md. Sultanul Islam
