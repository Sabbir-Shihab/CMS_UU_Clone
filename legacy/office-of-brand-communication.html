Office of Brand & Communication

The Office of Brand and Communication at Uttara University is responsible for overseeing the university's communication initiatives and protecting its brand identity. By maintaining an unwavering dedication to strategic messaging, unified branding, and efficient communication, our department significantly contributes to the enhancement of the university's standing, prominence, and interaction with both internal and external stakeholders.

Central to our mission is the aim of fortifying and advancing the brand value and reputation of the university. Our team works conscientiously to create and execute all-encompassing brand strategies, messaging frameworks, and visual guidelines that effectively communicate the university's distinctive identity, strengths, and values to diverse target audiences.

The Office of Brand and Communication assumes a diverse array of primary duties, which comprise brand administration, marketing, public relations, and communication with both internal and external stakeholders. Our organisation works in close collaboration with university leadership, academic departments, and administrative units to guarantee that our efforts are in line with the strategic goals and objectives of the university.

In addition, our office is tasked with the development and implementation of integrated marketing and communication campaigns to promote the programmes, initiatives, and accomplishments of Uttara University. By utilising an assortment of communication channels—digital media, print materials, events, and public relations initiatives—we are able to effectively engage and reach our target audiences.

The Office of Brand and Communication serves a crucial function not only in its external communication endeavours but also in promoting internal engagement and communication among members of the university community. In order to maintain faculty, staff, and students informed and engaged regarding university news, events, and initiatives, we formulate and implement communication strategies and initiatives.
