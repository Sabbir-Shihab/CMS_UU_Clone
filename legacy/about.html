About UU

Uttara University is a centre of excellence for higher education, situated in the capital city of Dhaka in Bangladesh. In 2003, Uttara University initiated its journey with the hands of DAR Trust pursuant to the Private University Act 1992. Regular classes started in 2003 at the first campus which was located in Sector 6, Uttara Model Town, Dhaka. The university was housed in a dozen of individual campuses spanning across Dhaka city and were located in Uttara, Dhanmondi, Farmgate and Mirpur towns. These campuses were later consolidated and UU relocated to its state-of-the-art permanent campus on 1st July 2023 in greater Uttara. In the year 2024, it had secured a place for the first time in international ranking. The university offers several graduate and undergraduate programs through its five schools and fourteen departments. Over the years, this university has evolved into a comprehensive institution that provides a variety of research opportunities. Its motto, “Excellence in Higher Education and Research,” has earned it recognition on an international scale. The quality of education provided by Uttara University is reflected in the employment placements of its graduates and in its research output. In this cosmopolitan smart-city campus, students have the opportunity to demonstrate their potential to face global challenges in the national and international labour market. The primary goal of this institution is to facilitate the professional and personal development of its students. These students are consistently nurtured and assisted by the university in their preparation for challenging corporate environments, as well as in the enhancement of their professional and rational abilities. The medium of instruction for all academic programs at Uttara University is English. The academic year of the university incorporates three semester. Uttara University is an independent private organisation with its own governing body known as the board of trustees.

Uttara University was established in 2003 under the Dr M Azizur Rahman (DAR) Trust which is a non-profit and non-political entity founded in the year 1997. It has been funded and led by eminent educationist, economist, renowned entrepreneur and Freedom Fighter Dr M Azizur Rahman, and was incorporated in accordance with the guidelines of the Private University Act 1992 (revised in 2010) and by the instructions of the Ministry of Education and University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh. A former Economic Advisor to the United States Agency for International Development (USAID), Prof. Dr. M Azizur Rahman is the chief sponsor of the trust. Due to the philanthropic subsidy and gratitude of the Founder, UU has been able to offer the country’s most desirable degree programs. His envision of a great educational voyage has changed lives and communities crossing borders. He is also the founder Vice-Chancellor of Uttara University. A PhD in Economics from Vanderbilt University, a Nobel Laureate institution in the United States of America, Dr. Rahman is capable of bringing innovation to national education, which is advantageous for students who aspire to establish a sustainable career in the contemporary global marketplace. Dr. Rahman established a remarkable number of educational institutions in Bangladesh where meritorious and ardent students are benefited by their merit with qualitative ingredients through innovative programs. As he is also a columnist, he employs this approach to address urgent national and international concerns for a broad audience. Dr. Rahman has authored numerous publications that have been published both domestically and internationally in indexed journals. The Chancellor of Uttara University is the Honourable President of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

The Honourable Vice-Chancellor, who serves as the University's Chief Academic and Executive Officer, serves as the Head of the Syndicate and Academic Council. The Vice-Chancellor is supported by the Pro Vice-Chancellor and a Treasurer in order to enhance the appropriate administrative and academic activities which are governed by the Board of Trustees. On the recommendation of the UU Trustee Board, the VC, Pro VC and Treasurer have been appointed by the Honourable Chancellor and President of the country. In accordance with the Private University Act 2010, the Vice-Chancellor is obligated to oversee the administration and management of academic and administrative affairs by adhering to the policies and guidelines established by the UU Trustee Board.

The first Pro-Vice-Chancellor of Uttara University was Prof. Dr. Eaysmin Ara Lekha, who is currently the Vice-Chancellor of the institution. She was designated Pro-Vice-Chancellor by the then Hon'ble Chancellor of this University in recognition of her meritorious services and experience in the field of education. Previously, she served as the Dean of the School of Education and Physical Education and the Professor and Head of the Department of Education prior to her appointment as Pro-Vice-Chancellor. A PhD alumnus of Jadavpur University, India and Doctorate of Literature from Ranchi University, India, she is also the author of numerous books and founder of few educational institutions in Bangladesh. Sabira Rouf College, is one of the institutions that Dr. Lekha has established as a patron of learning.

Overview

Uttara University (UU) is a government approved private university founded in 2003 by Dr. M Azizur Rahman. The University is an independent organization with its own Board of Trustees. The University received its initial Approval for Establishment and Operation by the Government of Bangladesh on 28.5.2003, available HERE.

Since its inception in 2003, Uttara University (UU) has been committed to fostering academic excellence and innovation. With a state-of-the-art permanent campus and five schools, UU offers a dynamic educational environment catering to our students' diverse needs. Our academic calendar consists of two semesters annually, ensuring a well-rounded and comprehensive learning experience across our 14 departments and 39 programs.

At Uttara University, we prioritize access to resources, boasting the dedicated M Azizur Rahman Library to support our students' academic endeavors. Additionally, our university is home to a dozen UU Centers and comprising 56 labs. These facilities include specialized labs within departments such as Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Civil Engineering (CE), Fashion Design and Technology (FDT), and Textile Engineering (TXE), providing students with hands-on learning opportunities in their respective fields.

Uttara University's instruction medium is English, facilitating unified communications and collaborations among our diverse student body. Our commitment to research is evident through our esteemed UU Research Centers, including the Center for Research & Training (CRT), Center for Sustainable Nanoparticle Synthesis (CSNS), Center for Clean Energy Research (CCER), and the Fintech Research Group (FRG) Moreover, our university boasts cutting-edge UU Labs, such as the Industrial Automation & Robotics Lab [IARLab], Generative Artificial Intelligence Lab [GAILab], Health Humanities Lab [HHLab], Innovation & Incubation Lab [IILab] and Digital Language Lab [DLLab]. These facilities are hubs for innovation and exploration, empowering our students to engage in groundbreaking research and discovery.

Facilities

Uttara University (UU) boasts a highly secured campus, ensuring the safety and well-being of all students, faculty, and staff members. Alongside this commitment to security, we offer personalized supervision and comprehensive career counseling facilities to guide students on their academic and professional journey. Additionally, our dedicated placement service assists students in securing promising career opportunities upon graduation, facilitating a smooth transition into the workforce. Within our campus, students have access to a vibrant cafeteria offering delicious and nutritious meals to suit diverse tastes and dietary preferences. Furthermore, regular job fairs provide invaluable networking opportunities, connecting students with potential employers and enhancing their career prospects. For moments of reflection and spiritual practice, a serene prayer room is available to both male and female students, fostering a supportive and inclusive campus environment.

Academic transparency and integrity is a priority at our university, and we are committed to the timely publication of results and ensuring students have access to accurate and up-to-date academic records. Our vibrant alumni association office enables graduates to stay connected with their peers and tap into a valuable professional network, facilitating ongoing career development and worldwide support. In line with technological advancements, our university offers modern Information Technology facilities, including My UU ERP, broadband internet, Wi-Fi, state-of-the-art lab facilities, and a dynamic presence across various platforms, both online and offline. Specialized lab facilities, such as computer labs, textile labs, civil labs, and electrical & electronics labs, provide students with hands-on learning and research opportunities.

Our rich library boasts vast resources, complemented by online databases, journal subscriptions and open-source software like Koha, empowering students with comprehensive academic support. Furthermore, students can engage in various sports and club activities, promoting physical fitness, teamwork, leadership and personal growth. Comfort and focus are prioritized in our air-conditioned classrooms, ensuring an optimal learning environment conducive to academic success.

Our university offers seamless overseas credit transfer facilities for students seeking international exposure, allowing them to broaden their academic horizons and explore new educational opportunities. Additionally, diverse seminars, workshops, and local and international conferences enrich the academic experience, expanding students' knowledge base and fostering intellectual growth.
