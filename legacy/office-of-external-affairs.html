Office of External Affairs

The Office of External Affairs at Uttara University functions as an essential intermediary connecting the institution with its external stakeholders, which comprise industry partners, government agencies, alumni, and the wider community. By placing an emphasis on cultivating alliances, encouraging cooperation, and bolstering the university's standing and exposure, our department significantly contributes to the realisation of Uttara University's strategic aims and objectives.

The Office of External Affairs (OEA) of Uttara University was established in January 2024. Its main objective is to enhance the reputation of the university both nationally and internationally. The OEA is currently divided into two divisions and operates under the supervision of the Vice-Chancellor: Centre for Career Development (CCD) and Office of the Alumni Affairs (OAA).

Vision

With the motto of UU, the OEA is dedicated to equipping graduates with high levels of soft skills, making them fit for the job market through seminars, training sessions, symposiums, and workshops.

Mission

The OEA of UU will facilitate mutual collaboration among students, faculty members, and alumni by providing a digital platform for its global alumni to stay connected and exchange international experiences.
