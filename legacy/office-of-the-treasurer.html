Message from Treasurer

Professor Haspia Bashirullah

"Education is simply the soul of the society as it passes from one generation to another" – G.K. Chesterton.

Uttara University was established in 2003. The vision of the founder and first Vice Chancellor Prof. Dr. M. Azizur Rahman was to establish a student society that is highly efficient, disciplined, patriotic and above all successful in every sphere of life. The mission was to bring higher education to the reach of those who cannot afford it. With the slogan “Quality Education at affordable tuition", Uttara University began its journey by opening its doors to the underprivileged and financially constraint students. In its 25 years history, Uttara University has changed the lives of hundreds of students and made their dreams come true.

The University is located near Uttara North Metro Rail Station. Thus it is accessable from every area of Dhaka City. It also provides a free bus service for students both inside Dhaka City and its outskirts.

The campus of Uttara University has a very modern architecture. It offers world class facilities such as classrooms equipped with latest technology, digital library, study area, computer lab accessibility for all students, indoor games area, canteen, etc. Students studying here can have a “home away from home”, feeling while they stay in the campus.

The teaching faculty is not only highly qualified in their respective fields but also have a student friendly attitude. The mental and physical health of the students are supported and maintained by different activity clubs, such as reading club, sports club, blood doner's club, wanderlust club, heritage club, and many more clubs. Mental health lab, digital language lab, and other science and engineering labs teach students to improve their subject skills through practical classes.

Students in Uttara University learn in both inside the classroom as well as in outside action research programs. The focus of Uttara University now is "Excellence in higher education and Research".

Study in Uttara University is not only an opportunity but also a privilege.
