Vice Chancellor's Message

Prof. Dr. Eaysmin Ara Lekha

Uttara University welcomes you. We hope that learning about us from the website will inspire you to visit us in person one day.

Hard work, academic excellence, and a commitment to holistic development have enabled Uttara University to become the professional educational entity it is today. This did not happen overnight; it is more than a two decade long journey. When state institutions had limited admission capacity, Uttara University was established to provide excellent education at reasonable tuition rates. It has since helped thousands of students achieve their professional goals and contribute to the nation's progress.

With "Excellence in Higher Education and Research" as its motto, Uttara University has evolved into a multi-faculty institution. It is now a hub for research, innovation and education. The university supports outstanding researchers with international conference funding and annual research awards for research publications. Years of perseverance have enabled us to enter the worldwide university rankings for the first time in 2024, starting with the World University Rankings for Innovations. We have already made our mark on the global higher education scene, and in due time, we will be included in all other reputable rankings and earn further accreditation.

In addition to our academic efforts, Uttara University strives to demonstrate the importance of sports and physical activities in the country by pioneering the first Physical Education program in the country. We offer a large number of programs and some of them are very unique in terms of curriculum design. By collaborating with the university’s board of trustees and an international advisory board, the institution ensures that its curriculum remains up-to-date and aligns with the ideals and aspirations of Generation Z. The Founder's Scholarship grants meritorious students free education. Co-curricular programs on job interview skills, AI, blockchain, language, engineering and startups highlight the school's leadership development.

May Allah bless our institution of Uttara University and our nation.
