Dean's Message

The School of Education and Physical Education is one of the five Schools at Uttara University, and it started its activity in 2003. The School is committed to quality education services. The teachers of this school maintain ethical standards. The programs profoundly provide knowledge of physical education, sports science, and pedagogical knowledge in the education system that enables the students to develop an understanding of the contributions of various schools of philosophy to the field of education. Preparing educational leaders, managers, educationists, researchers, and curriculum developers for the nation is one of the missions of the education department. The objectives of the development of physical education are to foster the moral, emotional, physical, behavioral, social, and academic development of the youth to concentrate on both national and international levels.
