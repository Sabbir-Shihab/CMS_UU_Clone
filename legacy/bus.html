UU Bus Service

Home / About / UU Bus Service

Reliable transportation is essential for students to arrive at their classes on time. While the majority of university students are able to commute to the campus on their own, they are dependent on public transportation.

Since UU considers every concern regarding students, we have a bus service to address the situation. We have around 40+ buses that are primarily reserved for students, as well as faculty and staff.

Our campus serves as the central hub for managing our entire transportation system. Students can access transportation services from Turag to various routes and campuses. Students must present their ID cards to avail the free service. A total of various destinations are covered with multiple trips to each location. This bus service is designed to prioritize the safety of female students, offering a reliable and reassuring transportation option.

Details

  1. The university runs shuttle buses from the permanent campus.
  2. The shuttle service enables students to travel to the permanent campus, and there are various routes.

Benefits of the University Bus Service

  1. Students can arrive consistently on schedule
  2. Student safety is prioritized while travel time is minimized
  3. It contributes to a healthier and cleaner environment by minimizing carbon emissions
  4. Traffic congestion is alleviated near the campus
  5. It offers a more affordable transportation option compared to personal vehicles
  6. Reduces the amount of parking space required for students' vehicles
