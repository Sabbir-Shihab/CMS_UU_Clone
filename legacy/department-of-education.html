Bachelor of Education (BEd)

Bachelor of Education (BEd) program is of 1 year duration divided into 3 semesters. Graduates of all streams of Education working in different educational institutes or/and in education related fields are eligible for admission in this program. Pedagogical courses are emphasis in this Program. The program is of 44 credit hours of consisting of 15 credits hours of Education Science Course, 6 credit hours of General Education Course, 6 credit hours of Teacher Training with Subject Specialization course, 15 credit hours of Practicum and 2 credit hours for Comprehensive exam. Practicum is the special feature of this program that includes Micro Teaching and Simulation (3 credit hours) and internship (12 credit hours). After completion of the program the students are expected to be capable of imparting quality teaching in educational activities and contribute in the development of the learned society in the Country.

Master of Education (MEd)

Master of Education (MEd) is a 1-year program by coursework involves taught courses of 41 credits. This program helps extension of knowledge obtained from Bachelor of Education programs. Courses studied here are pedagogical courses and general education courses with the subject specialization courses. This program aims to prepare educational leaders who can contribute to the enhancement of quality in education sector of the country.

Why Uttara University is one of the best choices for studying Education?

Uttara University stands out as one of the premier choices for studying Education in Bangladesh because of its pioneering heritage, modern curriculum, and deep commitment to student accessibility. As one of the earliest private institutions to establish a dedicated School of Education, it offers decades of specialized institutional expertise that balances foundational pedagogical theory with rigorous, hands-on classroom training. Its programs utilize an Outcomes-Based Education (OBE) framework heavily integrated with Information and Communication Technology (ICT), ensuring graduates are fully equipped to navigate and lead in today's increasingly digital academic landscapes. Furthermore, Uttara University’s extensive network of partner schools provides students with invaluable internship opportunities that often translate directly into employment. When combined with its highly affordable tuition structures and exceptionally generous merit-based scholarships, including up to a 100% waiver for top achievers, the university offers an unmatched blend of academic excellence, practical career readiness, and financial flexibility for aspiring educators and school administrators alike.

Overview of the Department

The Department of Education is dedicated to cultivating innovative teaching practices and fostering intellectual growth. With a commitment to excellence in pedagogy, our department provides comprehensive programs designed to prepare educators, administrators, and researchers to meet the diverse needs of learners in today's complex educational landscape. Through rigorous coursework, experiential learning opportunities, and close mentorship, we empower our students to become reflective practitioners equipped with the knowledge, skills, and dispositions necessary to effect positive change in educational settings. Our department is dedicated to advancing education at the national level and promoting social justice through transformative learning experiences. Preparing good citizens is also one of the emphasized areas of our department.

Vision

Transforming students through quality teaching and learning, along with innovative practices, into high-quality and committed professionals for various specialized areas of education who will understand the significance of cultural diversity and become agents of social transformation toward equity and excellence.

Mission

The mission of the Department of Education is to -

M1 : produce competent classroom teachers for schools of secondary level;

M2 : prepare educational leaders, managers, educationists, researchers and curriculum developers for the nation;

M3 : improve quality of teachers through pre-service and in-service teacher education;

M4 : motivate the youths towards educational professions;

M5 : develop linkages with the national and international academic communities;

M6 : take equity measures and address the needs of the divergent learners effectively;

M7 : develop educational leadership, ICT and entrepreneurship in education.

What Expertise Do We Build Up?

The Department of Education works on several key areas essential for success in the field of education:

  • Focus on cultivating proficiency in implementing innovative teaching methodologies tailored to meet the evolving needs of learners in diverse educational environments.
  • Emphasize the development of strong pedagogical foundations, ensuring educators possess the necessary skills to engage students effectively and facilitate meaningful learning experiences.
  • Through experiential learning opportunities, students gain practical insights and hands-on experience, refining their ability to navigate real-world challenges in educational settings.
  • Prepare graduates as ambassadors of Uttara University to drive positive change within the education sector.

Objectives

The objectives of the Department are to:

  • Develop the perception of learners on the discipline of education as a lifelong activity.
  • Facilitate learners to participate actively in the educational development of society.
  • Prepare learners for content excellence, pedagogical and ICT competence, and commitment.
  • Develop learners in curriculum development, management skills, and research.
  • Make learners competent in evaluation and assessment techniques.
  • Ensure learners’ moral and ethical education.

Medium of Instruction

Uttara University offers all the courses of study primarily in English. Teachers provide instruction to students in the classroom in English. English is also widely used as a medium of communication among the faculties, students, and administrative officials.

Bachelor of Education (BEd) Program (1 Year)

Description of the Program

The Bachelor of Education (BEd) program is of 1-year duration divided into 3 semesters. Graduates of all streams of education working in different educational institutes and/or in education-related fields are eligible for admission to this program. Pedagogical courses are emphasized in this program. The program consists of 44 credit hours, including 15 credit hours of Education Science courses, 6 credit hours of General Education courses, 6 credit hours of Teacher Training with Subject Specialization courses, 15 credit hours of Practicum, and 2 credit hours for the Comprehensive Exam at the end of the second semester. Practicum is the special feature of this program that includes Micro Teaching and Simulation (3 credit hours) and an internship (12 credit hours). After completion of the program, students are expected to be capable of imparting quality teaching in educational activities and contributing to the development of a learned society in the country.

Graduate Attributes

The graduates will be able to:

  • Develop in-depth knowledge of general and pedagogical courses, thereby attaining quality in the field of education.
  • Explain and apply theories and concepts related to educational issues.
  • Acquire special knowledge and professional skills in education.
  • Analyze critically and creatively issues in education from different perspectives.
  • Demonstrate competence in research in education.
  • Develop a reflective and multidisciplinary approach to education.
  • Undertake educational initiatives and adopt newer ideas in education.
  • Express national priorities for education and aspirations for the future of the nation.
  • Exhibit teamwork and leadership in education.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Learners will be able to -

PEO1Enhance the quality of classroom teaching by conveying analytical knowledge and critical understanding in education from different perspectives.
PEO2Reflect on a multidisciplinary approach to education in the context of social, cultural, economic, spiritual, behavioral, and professional viewpoints.
PEO3Initiate research activities and promote leadership qualities in education to meet national aspirations.
PEO4Develop skills and competencies to achieve professionalism and fulfill assigned responsibilities both at home and abroad.
PEO5Help develop competency in education and foster innovation.

Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

After completing the BEd the learners will be able to:

PLO1Able to execute good teaching, develop mastery of content, solve problems, and make decisions.
PLO2Imbue the spirit of nationalism, dedication, and attain the spirit of internationalism.
PLO3Manage classroom activities successfully for sustainable learning.
PLO4Plan and execute all sorts of co-curricular activities.
PLO5Provide counseling and guidance services to learners efficiently.
PLO6Cater to the educational needs of society.

Bachelor of Education (BEd) Program (4 Year)

Description of the Program

Bachelor of Education (Hons.) program is of 4 years duration, divided into 12 semesters. This program consists of 140 credit hours, including 48 credit hours for Core Courses, 36 credit hours for General Education Courses, 27 credit hours for Elective Courses, 9 credit hours for Teacher Training with Subject Specialization, 4 credit hours for Demonstration/Simulation and Micro Teaching, 12 credit hours for Practice Teaching (Internship), and 4 credit hours for Viva-voce. Its aim is to develop learners’ knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values, as well as their ability in research using appropriate methods and techniques. The program is expected to facilitate the achievement of theoretical and practical knowledge regarding the social, emotional, mental, physical, and cognitive development of each student, regardless of gender, race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status, or geographic location.

Graduate Attributes

The graduates will be able to:

  • Develop in-depth knowledge of education.
  • Express national priorities for education and aspirations for the future of the nation.
  • Explain and apply theories and concepts in educational issues.
  • Acquire specialized knowledge and professional skills in education.
  • Analyze critically and creatively issues in education from different perspectives.
  • Demonstrate competence in research in education.
  • Develop a reflective and multi-disciplinary approach to education.
  • Undertake educational initiatives and adopt newer ideas in education.
  • Exhibit teamwork and leadership in education.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Learners will be able to -

PEO1Acquire general and pedagogical knowledge to disseminate them in educational and other practical areas and act upon oral and written communication using language effectively for social, academic and professional purposes.
PEO2Understand social and national problems and attain the capability to solve these issues. Gain competencies in employment and entrepreneurship, contribute ethically to society, achieve the concept of free will, develop critical thinking, acquire inquisitiveness, and attain human qualities.
PEO3Contribute to improving teaching strategies in our country and solve complex tasks using advanced digital literacy.
PEO4Acquire quality education leading to higher studies and educational research, and become conscious of the independence and sovereignty of Bangladesh.
PEO5Become aware of race, religion, creed, and communalism, fostering friendliness, global fraternity, fellow-feeling, and respect for human rights.

Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

Learners will be able to:

PLO1Demonstrate knowledge and critical understanding of educational and other courses.
PLO2Apply underlying principles of sociological, philosophical, historical and psychological thoughts of education in personal life and in professional contexts.
PLO3Show five skills of communication (Seeing, Reading, Writing, Speaking, and Listening); communicate and interact effectively and spontaneously in Bangla and English for social, academic and professional purposes.
PLO4Perform advanced digital literacy in solving complex tasks.
PLO5Produce texts on school subjects and other practical courses and demonstrate the ability of entrepreneurs and innovative skills in planning daily activities.
PLO6Make self-development, and demonstrate proper knowledge and skills both in general and technical areas.
PLO7Demonstrate social, ethical, environmental and professional values.

Admission Requirements (Graduate)

Bachelor of Education with Honours (BEd Hons) (1 Year)

Structure of the Curriculum

a) Duration of the program: 3 semesters (1 year)

During a semester, each course will meet for 3 hours a week for the 3 credit courses. Additional classes or sessions in the form of discussion sessions, seminars, workshops, practical work, and fieldwork may be scheduled by the respective course instructors.

Semesters: The Uttara University has three semesters in an academic year- January-April, May-August and September-December

b) Admission Requirements: Graduates from any discipline (Science, Arts, and Commerce) may apply for admission to the BEd program. Preference will be given to those with an association with teaching. In addition, candidates must be physically and mentally fit, and they must qualify in the written test and viva.

c) Total minimum credit requirement to complete the program: 44 Credits

d) Total class weeks in a semester: 14 Weeks

e) Minimum CGPA requirements for graduation: 2.0

f) Maximum academic years of completion: (1+2) = 03 years

g) Category of Courses: 04

Courses offered by Uttara University for BEd are grouped into five. They are:

  • Education Science Courses: 5 Courses; 3 x 5 = 15 Credits
  • General Education Courses: 2 Courses; 2× 3 = 6 Credits
  • Teacher Training with Subject Specialization: 2 Courses; 3+12= 15 Credits
  • Comprehensive Exam: 1 Comprehensive Exam; 1 x 2 = 2 Credits

Total =15+6+6+15+2 = 44 Credits

Education Science courses, General Education courses, Practicum courses, and the Comprehensive Exam are compulsory for all trainees. Teacher training with subject specialization (elective/concentration courses) offers trainees the choice to select subjects based on their educational background and professional needs. These elective courses have been designed with consideration of the similar courses in our secondary education system. To help teachers achieve in-depth knowledge of their school subject, they will not be allowed to select from groups other than their own.

Master of Education (MEd)

Structure of the Curriculum

a) Duration of the program: 3 semesters (1 year) for coursework and 3 semesters for mixed-mode. Each academic year is divided into three semesters.

b) Admission Requirements: For admission to the MEd program, the minimum qualification is graduation with a pedagogical degree. In addition to this, candidates must be physically and mentally fit, and they must qualify in the written test and viva.

c) Total minimum credit requirement to complete the program: 41 Credits for both Coursework and Mixed-mode.

d) Total class weeks in a semester: 14 Weeks/Semester

e) Minimum CGPA requirements for graduation: 2.0

f) Maximum academic years of completion: 03 Years

g) Category of Courses:

Category for Coursework :-

  • Number of Core Courses = 10 (30 Credits)
  • Number of GED Courses = 2 (6 Credits)
  • Dissertation = 1 (3 Credits)
  • Viva-Voce = 1 (2 Credits)

Category for Mixed-mode :-

  • Number of Core Courses = 5 (15 Credits)
  • Number of GED Courses = 2 (6 Credits)
  • Thesis = 1 (20 Credits)

Admission Requirements (Undergraduate)

Bachelor of Education with Honours (BEd Hons) (4 Years)

Structure of the Curriculum

a) Duration of the program: 04 Years

Semesters: twelve semesters. Three semesters in an academic year- January-April, May-August and September-December

b) Admission Requirements: Students can be admitted to the BEd (Hons) program in the Education Department if they have obtained a minimum CGPA of 2.50 in both SSC and HSC or equivalent examination levels separately, or a total of 6.0 points in both cases with a minimum of 2.0 in one level, as per UGC rules. Candidates will be selected for admission based on a written test and a viva voce. Admission tests will be arranged by the School/Department or the university. Students with a break in their studies may apply, and they must obtain qualifying marks in the admission test.

c) Total minimum credit requirement to complete the program: 140 Credits

d) Total class weeks in a year/semester: 14 weeks in a semester / 28 weeks in a semester Additional classes or sessions in the form of discussion sessions, seminars, workshops, practical and field works etc., may be scheduled by the respective course instructors.

e) Minimum CGPA requirements for graduation: 2.0 credits

f) Maximum academic years of completion: (4+3) = 7 Years

g) Category of Courses:

  • Core Courses: 16 Courses x 3 Credits = 48 Credits
  • General Education Courses: 12 Courses x 3 credits = 36 Credits
  • Courses from any elective area of Specialization: 9 Courses x 3 Credits = 27 Credits
  • Courses from Teacher Training with Subject Specialization: 03 Courses x 3 Credits = 9 Credits
  • Practicum (16 Credits)
    1. Demonstration/Simulation and Microteaching = 4 Credits
    2. Practice Teaching (Internship) = 12 Credits
  • Viva-voce: 04 Credits

Total = 48+36+27+9+16+4 = 140 Credits
