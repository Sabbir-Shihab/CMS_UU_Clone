Advisory Board

The Advisory Board at Uttara University is committed to furthering the university's mission Excellence In Higher Education And Research by serving as a beacon of strategic leadership and visionary guidance. Owing to its steadfast dedication to cultivating excellence, collaboration, and innovation, the Advisory Board significantly influences the university's long-term success and strategic trajectory.

Driven by an unwavering dedication to maintaining the utmost levels of academic integrity and institutional governance, the Chief Advisor assumes the pivotal role of counsel to the university administration, providing sage advice and strategic guidance on academic policy matters, strategic initiatives, and institutional development.

Our Advisory Board upholds the highest standards of higher education by acting as a stronghold of insight and prediction, guaranteeing that Uttara University maintains its leading position in terms of progress and societal contribution.
